:: A Bit About This Walkthrough                                         abt ::
==============================================================================

First off, thank you for looking at my walkthrough.  I have made it possible
to skip around using the codes found in the table of contents.  I have also
tried very hard to not give up any spoilers.  Some of the trophies are
character specific, so you will mostly likely have to do more than one play
through if you wish to earn them all.  One such example is Dagger of the Mind
where you will need to mind meld with ten different enemies while playing as
Spock.

Otherwise, the game is pretty straightforward.  I have included in this
walkthrough locations of resesarch data as you progress through the levels.
Many of them are found in side rooms or away from the main direction of game
play.  Good luck and I hope you enjoyed this game as much as I did.  Well, at
least as much as the first time I played it.

:: Controls                                                                 ::
==============================================================================
: ct1                                                           Opening Menu :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D-Pad/Left Analog Stick - Scroll/Adjust options
X                       - Select/Toggle
O                       - Cancel/Back
R2/L2                   - Scroll through options (in Options menu)
[]                      - Reset to defaults (in Options menu)
Start                   - Second player join

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
: ct2                                                                In-Game :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Left Analog Stick  - Move character
Right Analog Stick - Camera
D-Pad              - Weapon select
Select             - Tricorder and weapon upgrade screen
Start              - Pause/In-game menu
L1 (Hold)          - Crosshairs
R1                 - Weapon primary fire
R2                 - Weapon secondary fire
L2 (Hold)          - Tricorder
L3                 - Sprint (while moving)
R3                 - Crouch
Triangle           - Tell partner where to move (while holding down L2)
                     Switch to partner's equipment (while in upgrade screen)
Square             - Action
X                  - Jump
O                  - Drop/Get behind cover

==============================================================================
:: Menus                                                                    ::
==============================================================================
: menu1                                                                 Main :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Continue - Available after completing the game once
New Game - Start a new game
Chapters - Select to choose from selected save points
Online   - Signs you in with your PSN ID to play online
Options  - Adjust gameplay, sound, and viewing options here
Credits  - Plays the ending credits

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
: menu2                                                              In-Game :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Resume          - Continue
Research Data   - Displays information on items you scan
Last Checkpoint - Returns you to the last checkpoint determined by the game
Restart Level   - Returns you to the beginning of the particular level
Options         - Same as the one in the Main menu
Quit            - Returns you to the main menu

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
: menu3                                                            No Health :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Try Again     - Return to the last save point
Restart Level - Returns you to the beginning of the level
Quit          - Returns you to the main menu

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
: menu4                                                               Online :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Quick Match   - Joins any public game online once your partner reaches a
                checkpoint
Custom Match  - Start the game from any chapter
Check Invites - Check to see if you received any messages

==============================================================================
:: Game Types                                                               ::
==============================================================================

Public  - Anyone can join your mission at any time.
          Requires you to be online.  Need to have a PSN ID.
Private - No one can join your mission, unless you invite them.
          Requires you to be online.  Need to have a PSN ID.
Offline - Play on your own.  A second player can drop in at any time locally.

: wt01                                                              Prologue :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                  Boldly Go |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You begin in a firefight.  The on-screen instructions show you what you need
to do.  Hold down L1 for the crosshairs to show up and press R1 to fire.  The
bottom left of the screen shows you a heat monitor for your weapon.  If it
fills up, you will not be able to fire until your weapon has cooled down.
After a little bit, the game will take over, so be confused after watching the
cutscene.

| Objectives :: Reach the Bridge                                             |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the cutscene, make your way to the bridge.  Follow the red circle.

circle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Move to the centre of the scanner for decontamination        |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do like the objective says, which you will have to do anyway for the door to

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Proceed to the Command Center                                |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                              Space Station |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                   | Reactor |
                                                                   -----------

Go through door for a cutscene.  With the bridge out, you will need to find an
alternate route.  Turn the camera so you can see to the left and make your way
to the red circle.  Use X to jump over the debris and hold square to enter the
vent at the end.

Make your way through and drop down the hole at the end.  Press circle to drop
down from the edge.  The door at the end of the hall here is stuck, so hold
down square and then tap square to pry open the door.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Unlock the door to free the Vulcan Scientist                 |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Head towards the locked door and hold down L2 to bring up tricorder view.
Target the lock and hold square to unlock the door.  Enter the room to save
the scientist.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Find a way to open the door                                  |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hold down L2 again and target the console that was in front of the Vulcan
scientist and hold square to hack it.  What you will need to do here is pair
up the frequencies.  You will need to use both analog sticks to move the
selectors.  Once you find matching frequencies, press X, doing this for four
pairs of frequencies.

In this room are two items you can scan.  One is just past the console you
just hacked and one is behind you next to another console.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Continue to the Command Center                               |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go through the now unlocked door to another cutscene.  Make your way up back
to the top.  Once there, use the tricorder to disable the laser arms.  Once
they are disabled, face back towards the door you came through initially and
jump up the arms to the level above.  Once up, pry open the door.  Press R3
to crouch and make your way across.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                              Space Shuttle |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                            | Command Center |
                                                            ------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Proceed to the Airlock to begin the Spacewalk                |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the cutscene, head to the turbolift.  You can scan the holographic image
before doing so.  Once out, turn left and you will see a communicator you can
scan.  If you go straight ahead to the damaged doors, on the other side is a
Tribble you can scan.  Head to the airlock.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                   Helios-1 |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                    2259.32 |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Proceed to the Station Core                                  |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the cutscene, you will need to make your way across while avoiding the
solar radiation.  You can use O to take cover behind objects, which will help
you avoid direct contact.  You can press L3 while you are running to sprint,
which helps you get across faster.

Once at the door, hold square, then tap square to pry open the door.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                              Space Station |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                      | Space Station's Core |
                                                      ------------------------

You will need to shut down the rings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Shut down the first Core ring                                |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Shut down the first ring.  Listen to your partner and you will need to
complete the other part.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Shut down the second Core ring                               |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do the same thing you did to shut down the first core.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Shut down the third Core ring                                |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Again, same as the first two cores.  This time around you will also need to
avoid the radiation blasts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Exit the Station Core                                        |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once again, you will be subject to radiation blasts, so carefully make your
way to the door the red circle is at and pry it open.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Proceed to the Emergency Transporter Room                    |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You will come up to some vents that you can blast that will help provide cover
for you to make it across.  However, some of them are actual vents that
provide no cover whatsoever.  Once you get across, you will view a cutscene.

You can scan the console, and just a little past it is a communicator you can
scan.

Make your way to the red circle for another cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Outrun the Destruction!                                      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Just sprint until the cutscene plays.

: wt03                                                             New Vulcan :
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Follow T'Mar                                                 |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do as the objective says and follow T'Mar.  By now, you may want to purchase
some upgrades.  Ones I recommend at this point are Techno-Doctor, Remote
Connector, and Research Helper for the Tricorder and Heat Efficient Shot for
the phaser.  Make Research Helper your first choice for now and Techno-Doctor
a second priority.  Research Helper will point out scannable items for you in
tricorder view if you are in range of them, which gives you experience points.

As you follow her, you can scan the plant life on the left, some grass on the
right just past a small rock, and a bird on the roof of the building you come
upon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Hack the door with your partner                              |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When you reach the door, you will notice two panels.  I suggest hacking the
one on the left as it is the easier of the two.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                          | Security Station |
                                                          --------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Find Surok                                                   |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go through the door and immediately to the left is another Tribble you can
scan.  Activate the panel across the way to extend the bridge.  Once across,
make your way to the door.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                          | Unfinished Tower |
                                                          --------------------

Inside, you can scan the body.  A little past it is a section of the wall you
can scan.  And a little past that is some kind of blood you can scan.  Stun
the enemy using R2, your weapon's secondary fire, and press square to knock
him out.  Enter the turbolift.

Take out the enemy out here then have Spock mind meld with him to obtain the
code for the door.  Unlock the door and go through.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                | Upper Labs |
                                                                --------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Commendation :: Complete your objectives without directly killing any      |
|              :: Infected (Non-Lethal)                                      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Note that you can also use the tricorder to view any enemies in the vicinity.
So, unlock the door and non-lethally take out the two infected Vulcans that
come running through the now unlocked door.  Hold the left analog stick
towards the broken glass and hold X to hop over.  Straight ahead is a console
you can scan.  Go through the door.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                               | Surok's Lab |
                                                               ---------------

Head towards the red circle for a cutscene.  At the back of the room is
another console you can scan.  Follow Surok to the door.  On the way, there
is another console that is scannable here.  Go through the door when you are
ready.

Just past the door, next to the console, is another communicator you can scan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Secure the Helios Device                                     |
|            :: Pick up the Power Cell 0/1                                   |
|            :: Install the Power Cell                                       |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You will need to restore power to a door using a power cell.  Have your
partner pick it up or you can pick it up and insert it into the port to
continue.  Take out the infected Vulcan inside the room and have Spock mind
meld with him to gain a pass code.  The pass code is for the storage room
inside the room the infected Vulcan was in.  Inside you will find a
communicator that is scannable.

When ready, make your way to the red circle in the next room, making sure to
dispatch any infected Vulcans you come along.  Unlock the next door and take
out the other infected Vulcan.

After a brief semi-garbled communication with Dr. McCoy, head downstairs and
dispatch yet another infected Vulcan.  You can scan the weapon he was holding.
Go through the door to a room with about four more infected Vulcans.

After you take them out, follow the red circle to a turbolift.  You may also
want to grab a second weapon for yourself and your partner before heading to
the turbolift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                | Helios Lab |
                                                                --------------

Exit the turbolift and go forward.  Behind the console to the left of the door
with the fire in front of it is a communicator you can scan.  Just to the
right of the door that looks like a giant target is a hatch.  Open it and go
down.  Go across and up the ladder.  At the top, tap square to open the hatch
and get ready for your first boss fight.

******************************************************************************
*                             Boss Fight: Unknown                            *
******************************************************************************

Usually what this boss will do first is head towards the power cells.  When he
grabs one, blast it and it will cause a bit of damage.  Basically, just run
around dodging his attacks and heal your partner when he needs it.  When not
dodging, be sure to use the second weapon you picked up to take down its life
bar.  When you lose health, your partner will come heal you.  You may need to
retry this boss a few times before defeating it.  Be sure to scan its body.

Once that thing is down, head through the door it destroyed.  Just inside to
the left is another communicator you can scan.  Follow the red circle.  You
will need to wait for a decontamination scanner to complete before you will be
allowed to proceed.  Go through for a cutscene.

When it is done, take care of the enemies here and be sure to scan their
bodies and weapons.  Head to the turbolift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Recover the Helios Device                                    |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Scan and pick up the stun grenades and replenish ammo as needed, then head
through the doors and assist the Vulcans under attack.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                | Lower Labs |
                                                                --------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Commendation :: Find an alternate path around the Gorn's ambush            |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Here is how to receive the commendation experience.  Just past where the
Vulcans were blown smithereens is a stack of crates.  Behind the crates is a
scannable item.  Climb up and hold square to go to the upper level.  Make your
way through and go left at the last fork.  Take out the Gorn once you are back
down to the lower level.  Across the room, in the corner is another item you
can scan.

Midway through the hall is a side room with a healing station and a
communicator on the middle table on the far side.  Continue to the next door.
There is an object on the wall you can scan.  Pry the door open.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                | Power Room |
                                                                --------------

If you purchased the remote connector, install it and deactivate the terminal
across the way to de-electrify the water.  Otherwise you will need to raise
the platforms you can target with your tricorder and climb across.  There is a
side room on the left if you need to replenish any ammo and there is also a
communicator in here.  Make your way across and through the door.

Climb onto the crates and hold square to request a boost.  Make your way
across and drop down into the next room.  Next to the thing that looks like a
giant speaker is another communicator.  Unlock the door and grab the power
cell from inside this room and place it in the empty port outside to unlock
the next door and enter the turbolift.

Exit for a cutscene, then be ready to deal with some enemies.  After another
cutscene, be ready for yet more enemies.  And then, yes, yet another cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Take out a few more enemies here, then you will need to get to the sick bay.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Heal Kirk                                                    |

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                    | Medbay |
                                                                    ----------

In the medical facility, place Kirk on the gurney.  If you are playing as
Kirk, you will need to hold off the incoming Gorn.  If you are playing as
Spock, you will need to repair Kirk's leg.

When his leg is healed, take care of any enemies left in the room.  Across
from the gurney on the other side of the room is a communicator.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Escape the Science Center                                    |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Make your way back to the great hall and take care of any Gorn you see in
here.  Be sure to scan the monument in the middle of the room.  There are two
parts to it.  Then head to the door and pry it open.  You will meet up with
T'Mar on the other side.  Head out and be ready for yet another fight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                                                                 New Vulcan |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                               | Landing Pad |
                                                               ---------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Survive the Gorn Reinforcements                              |
|            :: Call down an Orbital Strike using your tricorder             |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once you get to the midlevel area, using your tricorder, target an area and
press R1 to order a torpedo strike to clear out the area so you can be beamed
out.

Be sure to look out for the enemies coming from the great hall.  Hold them off
and you will be beamed up.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
: wt04                                                          Space Battle :
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This battle is separated into three parts.

In the first part, you will need to fight off enemy fighters.  Use R1 to use
your phasers.

In the second part, you will need to fight off the same enemies from the first
part along with torpedos and kamikaze fighters.  You can use your shields with
L2.  They can be pulsed, so if you get hit with a kamikaze fighter, tap L2 to
get them off you.  You will also have access to tactical mode by pressing
triangle.  In tactical, you can target enemy weak points with R1, which will
be fired upon.

In the third part, you get to pilot a torpedo to your target.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Speak to Commodore Daniels                                   |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Move towards Dr. McCoy and he will begin talking.  You can listen or keep on

him, enter the next room.  There is a console here you can scan.  Head forward

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Follow the escort to the command center                      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go ahead and follow your escort and you will be asked to relinquish your
weapons.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Drop off your weapons                                        |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go into one of the side rooms and hold square to put your weapon on the table.
Be sure to scan all the weapons in here.  Then continue to the command center
for a cutscene.  After the cutscene, be sure to scan the console and chair.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Go to the mess hall                                          |
|            :: Retrieve your weapons                                        |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go back and retrieve your weapons.  In the opposite room is a communicator.
Follow the red circle to the bottom room.  Hack the power grid to turn on the
fire suppression system so you can enter the turbolift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Meet up with the security team                               |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There a couple of crates in here that are openable.  Go ahead and loot them
and go through the door for a cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Clear the mess hall of the invading Gorn                     |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go ahead and clear out the mess hall and make your way to the red circle.  Be
sure to scan weapons and bodies as you go.  Before leaving the mess hall, go
the opposite way to the tables and at the end there is a communicator.
Through the door are a few more Gorn you will need to take care of.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Go to the bio labs                                           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go through the door, replenish health and ammo as needed, then use the
turbolift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Find Bones                                                   |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the cutscene, be sure to have the remote connector installed and
override the turret.  It will take care of the patrolling Gorn for you.  Don't
forget to scan the turret.  Once through the door, you will view a cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Commendation :: Find Bones without provoking any enemy attacks (Stealth)   |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In order to obtain this commendation, you will need to make sure that none of
your enemies turn red when in tricorder view.  Yellow is okay, but once they
turn red, you fail the commendation.  And be sure to knock them out.  So take
care of the two in this room.  One walks into the room you are hiding in.  You
can use the dot in the middle of your screen as your crosshairs and stun and
take him down.  The other one patrolling inside the room should not be that
difficult to take down either.  Scan their bodies after you take them down.

Patrolling the corridor through the other door is a drone.  It can be stunned
and hacked to be placed under your control.  However, it would probably be
safer to stun it and destroy it as it could be the reason you fail the
commendation by attacking any and all enemies, which include infected crew
members.  Scan it after you destroy it.

Past the corridor and through the door are a number of infected crew members.
It is possible to enter the next room without being detected if you are
careful enough.  If you have to, go ahead and knock out one of the infected
crew members.  Make your way to the far corner by the energy station
and hold square for a boost.

Once above, enter the vent and turn right first.  You will first need to
disable a turret.  There also happens to be an infected crew member patrolling
that area.  Be sure to drop down when he is walking away.  Make your way to
the pillar and when he is in the area where the pillar blocks the view of the
turret, stun and take him down.  This will insure that you can override the
turret without losing the commendation.

When you are done here, go back up to the vent and head towards the opposite
end and drop down to the bottom level.  In the middle of the room is a
scannable object.  Outside of the room, you should be able to see a Gorn
patrolling and an infected crew member with his back facing a pillar.  Wait
for the Gorn to go away from you and make your way to the infected crewman and
knock him out.  Enter the room he was "guarding" and unlock the panel on the
ceiling then go up.

Make your way across and make sure the patrolling infected crewman is out of
the room before dropping down.  Take him down before trying to exit the room.
There is a scannable object in this room.  Make your way across the room to
the next room and pry open the door to the storage room, where you will find
Dr. McCoy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives   :: Destroy the three Gorn devices 0/3                         |
| Commendation :: Complete your objectives without directly killing any      |
|              :: Infected (Non-Lethal)                                      |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the cutscene, you will need to disable three of the Gorn hacking
devices.  You can either hack them or just blow them to smithereens.  Note
that hacking them will give you experience.  You can always blow them up
afterwards.  Once you get out into the hallway, you will have 400 seconds to
disable all three.  So just make your way to the closest one to you and take
them out one by one.

The closest one is in the room just a little past the turret.  As you exit the
room, turn right and it will be the first door on the left.  Inside you will
find a drone and a Gorn.  Stun and hack the drone and take out the Gorn, then
you can hack the device.  Don't forget to scan the Gorn and his weapon.

Straight from the door in this room on the ceiling is a panel you can unlock.
If you go up through the vent here, it will take you to another room with
another scannable object.  Return to the previous room via the vent.

The next closest one is straight down the hall.  You will have to go through a
couple of infected crewmen to get to it.  This one will be guarded by a few
Gorn.  Take them out, then hack the device and move on to the last one.

Enter the storage room close to where you disabled the second device and
unlock the hatch on the ceiling and go up.  Enter the vent, make your way
across, and drop down.  Hack the door open and make your way to the final
device, which should be unguarded.  Hack the device to complete this
objective.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objective :: Reach the next laboratory section                             |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Head back towards the room you came from but go around it in the hall towards
another turret that you will need to override.  Be sure to install the remote
connector then get as close as you can to the opening without provoking the
turret and hack the panel to disable it.  Then you can proceed through the
door.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Commendation :: Complete your objectives without provoking any enemy       |
|              :: attacks (Stealth)                                          |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the cutscene, hack the mine and wait for the patrolling Gorn to come
back to set off the mine.  Go through the door.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objective :: Retrieve the samples for Bones' research 0/2                  |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Across the way to the right is a mine that a patrolling Gorn walks by.  Hack
it to get rid of that patrol.  Over to the left across the platform is another
mine you will need to hack, so it does not blow you up.  Above that mine is
another Tribble, so be sure to scan it.  Down below is the turret control
panel and a mine close to it.  You will need to hang off the side of the
platform in order to be able to target the mine to hack it.  The difficult
part about this area is that if you override the turret, you lose your
commendation.  So you will need to take out the Gorn before overriding the
turret.  If you have enough experience points for the sound decoy go ahead and

the left you can take that will teleport you to the level above.  Otherwise,
activate the console to extend the bridge and go across.  Use the teleporter
to go across and hack the left terminal for a cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Proceed to the Targeting Platform                            |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Take care of the Gorn that teleports in, then use the teleporter that he
arrived on.  This will teleport you to the upper level where you will need to
take care of a few Gorn.  If you need it, there is a health and ammo
replenishment station in a side room.  Head forward to one of the two side
rooms and use the teleporter inside to teleport to the lower level.  You
should be able to stealth take down the lone patrolling Gorn down here.  Scan
the tank when you are ready to continue and you will view a cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                        | Targeting Platform |
                                                        ----------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Proceed to the Targeting Platform                            |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Proceed to the door and hack the left panel.  Enter for a cutscene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Disconnect the Targeting Platform Cables 0/4                 |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Be sure to scan the giant eyeball supercomputer.  You will now have a time
limit of 400 seconds to disconnect the cables.  First thing you will need to
do is override the turrets.  Activate the small platforms so you have
something to hide behind and make your way to the left.  There will be a
control panel you can hack to override the turret down here.  Use the
teleporter go up one level and run around the turret to the corner 
where its control panel is and override it.  This will help dispatch some Gorn
that teleport here and allow you deactivate the console here, disconnecting
the first cable.

Take the teleporter closest to the turret control panel and deactivate the
console here to disconnect the second cable, then take care of any enemies up
here.  Move across the way and deactivate the console there to disconnect the
third cable.  You may run into more resistance here.  Take care of them and
hack any drones you have time to takeover.

Take the teleporter just past the third console and quickly run to the left,
where you will find a control panel to override the turret.  Do so, then
deactivate the last console.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Destroy the Gorn Targeting Platform                          |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The clock is still ticking, so make your way back to the upper level and head
to the teleporter between the second and third consoles you deactivated and
use it.  Hack the left console, then open fire where the red circle is.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|Objectives :: Proceed to the Core                                           |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Take the teleporter back down, turn around, scan the console, and head to the
door.  Use the teleporter there and you will view a cutscene.

Again, you will need to take out more enemies here, then follow the red circle

******************************************************************************
*                            Boss Fight: Gorn Brute                          *
******************************************************************************

This fight is actually similar to the fight with the Unknown back on New
Vulcan.  This time, it will be much easier since you are not surprise
attacked.

After the fight, head across the other bridge and take care of the patrol and
make your way to the next teleporter.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Ascend to the Mothership Core                                |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Make your way around the center and take care of the Gorn enemies up here.
Move forward and activate the console to extend the bridge.  Head to the
central platform and take out the Gorn that teleport in.  Be careful of the
extremely tall one.  His weapon is very strong and can dematerialize objects.
When they are all dead, scan the body of the really tall Gorn, then go to the
area they all teleported in from and hack the left panel of the terminal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives   :: Overload the plasma transducers 0/2                        |
| Commendation :: Complete your objectives without provoking any enemy       |
|              :: attacks (Stealth)                                          |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Start off by heading down to the left and hiding behind the rock there.  As
the Gorn makes his rounds, you can stun and take him down when his back is
turned at the console.  Now move forward and you hide behind the giant rock
with the symbol on it.  From here, you can stun and take down the Gorn that
patrols the platform in the middle of the room.  The best time to get him is
when he is patrolling the front side of the structure.  Move forward again to
the next giant rock with the symbol on it and you will need to take out this
next Gorn.  Perform a mind meld on him to obtain a pass code and use it on the
console.  Then take out the Gorn across the way and perform another mind meld
on it to obtain another pass code and use it on the console there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Destroy the Helios Device                                    |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go ahead and fire on the Helios Device.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Objectives :: Get to T'Mar                                                 |
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Head towards T'Mar for a cutscene.

******************************************************************************
*                            Boss Fight: Commander                           *
******************************************************************************

Now you get to fight the Gorn Commander.  Watch out for the four grenades he
fires, all at once.  Otherwise, this will be like a standard fight.  You will
also need to avoid the heat sinks that appeared after you overloaded the
plasma transducers.  He can also teleport, so be careful.  When his health
gets low enough, you will view a cutscene.

Enjoy the credits and stay tuned for a cutscene that leads into Star Trek:
Into Darkness.

                                  kill another enemy
Non-Lethal Master               : Took down 30 enemies in a non-lethal manner
One Man Army                    : Kill 3 enemies while your teammate (AI or
                                  co-op player) is in a downed state
Pillager Master                 : Kill 50 enemies with the Pillager
Plasma Grenade Master           : Kill 20 enemies with Plasma Grenades
Railer Master                   : Kill 50 enemies with the Railer
Ravage Master                   : Kill 50 enemies with the Ravager
Researcher                      : Found half of all research items

Technologic                     : Cleared 30 mini-games including co-operative
