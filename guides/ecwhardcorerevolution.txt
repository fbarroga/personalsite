----------------
Types of Matches
----------------

These are the matches in the Exhibition mode.

1-Player
  Vs - One on One
  Tag Team - Two on Two
  Tornado - 4-player Elimination Match
  One on Two - Player against two computer
  One on Three - Player against three computer
  Lumberjack - Two in the ring, two "Lumberjacks" outside
  3-Way Dance - Triple threat rules
  Battle Royal - 4 in the ring, win by tossing opponents over top rope
  Vs* - 3-man gauntlet
  Tag Team* - One player controls a team, computer controls two teams
  8-Man Tag* - 4 on 4
  Battle Royal* - Royal Rumble match
  4-Way Dance* - 4-player elimination match
  Stable Match* - 4 teams of 4
  3-Way Dance* - 3-player elimination match

2-Player
  Vs - One on one
  Tag Team - One tag team vs. another tag team, each controlled by one 
player
  Co-op Tag Team - Both players play on the same team
  Tornado - 4-player elimination match
  Co-op Tornado - Tag team elimination match
  1 on 2 - One player & computer take on another player
  Co-op 2 on 1 - Both players take on computer
  1 on 3 - One player & two computer take on another player
  Co-op 3 on 1 - Both players & computer take on computer
  Lumberjack - Same as 1-player
  3-Way Dance - Triple threat match
  Battle Royal - 4 in the ring, win by tossing opponents over top rope
  Vs* - 3-man gauntlet
  Tag Team* - One player controls one team, one player controls two 
teams
  Co-op Tag Team* - 3-team gauntlet, both players on same team
  8-Man Tag* - Eliminate 4 opponents, each player controls one team
  Co-op 8-Man Tag* - Eliminate 4 opponents, both players on same team
  Battle Royal* - Royal Rumble match
  4-Way Dance* - 4-player elimination match
  Stable Match* - 4 teams of four, two controlled by players, two by 
computer
  3-Way Dance* - 3-man elimination match

3-Player
  Tag Team - Two players on one team, one on another
  Tornado - 4-player elimination match
  2 on 1 - Two players against one player
  3 on 1 - Two players & computer against one player
  Co-op 3 on 1 - 3 players against computer
  3-Way Dance - Triple threat match
  Battle Royal - 4 in the ring, win by tossing opponents over top rope
  Tag Team* - Two on one team, one controlling two other teams
  8-Man Tag* - Two players on one team, one player controls other
  Battle Royal* - Royal Rumble Match
  4-Way Dance* - 4-player elimination match
  Stable Match* - 4 teams of 4, one team per player, one team for 
computer
  3-Way Dance* - 3-player elimination match

4-Player
  Tag Team - 2 on 2
  Tornado - 4-player elimination match
  3 on 1 - 3 players against one
  Battle Royal - 4-player elimination match
  Tag Team* - Two players on one team, two players controls two teams
  8-Man Tag* - Pair of players each control one team
  Battle Royal* - Royal Rumble Match
  4-Way Dance* - 4-player elimination match
  Stable Match* - 4 teams of 4, each player controls own team

----------------
Default Controls
----------------

U - Move player up
D - Move player down/Come down from turnbuckle
L - Move player left
R - Move player right
/\ - Punch/Use weapon
[] - Kick/Use weapon
X - Block/Reversal
O - Tie-up
L1 - Switch focus/Pick up or put down weapons
L2 - Climb turnbuckle/Leave or Enter ring/Climb cage
R1 - Break pin or submission hold
R2 - Run
O+[] - Taunt

----------------------
A Note About The Moves
----------------------

I'd just like to point out that I've listed the Finishing and Trademark 
moves for all the characters.  You won't be able to do these moves right 
away.  In order to do the Trademark moves, your opponent's health meter 
must be at least be yellow.  In order to do the Finishing moves, your 
opponent's health meter must be red.  No ifs, ands, or buts about it.
