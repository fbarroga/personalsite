Chapter 4

Fresh Ground Coffee

 

It had been two days since Toxic, Creamy, and Extacy had returned to the village of Lai Chi after failing to find Hop and Musubi. They were still waiting for Dust.

"What's taking him so long?" asked Creamy.

***

Dust had just entered the town of Bai Bai Bai and was making his way towards Bi Fo Yu. As he walked he caught sight of Hop. Anger welled up in him and he began to laugh hysterically. Hop looked in his direction, dropped what he was doing and started walking towards him.

When he had finally reached him, he stopped and Dust spoke. "Finally, it's been a long time."

"Yes, it has. Things didn't have to be this way."

"It's too late for that now. You'll die for killing my brother."

"Dust, I didn't kill Cloud. Toxic killed Cloud."

"Shut up! I don't want to hear it anymore!" After he said that, he attacked Hop using Shaolin Shadowboxing. Hop fought back using his Drunken Boxing.

Everybody came out of the inn to see what was happening outside. Just as Musubi came out, Hop rolled right into him.

"Whoa! You okay Hop?"

"Yep, doing good."

"Need some help?"

"Yeah, give me a push."

Musubi gave him a little push and Hop went flying head first into Dust, knocking the wind out of him. Dust gasped for air and struggled to his feet.

"Damn you Hop. I'll get you next time," he said and high-tailed it out of town.

"This isn't good," said Musubi. "Now they know where we are."

"So what do we do now?" asked Angel.

Hop looked at Musubi, then over at the others. "Now, we have to try to retake Shaolin Temple, ready or not."
