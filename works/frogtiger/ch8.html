Chapter 8

Honey Dipped Chocolate Chips

 

While Dew and Dust had gone down to the front gates, the others went around the Temple looking for the monks that had been left behind to fight the Danji Yi. Musubi had succeeded in finding them.

"Hey guys!" he yelled. "They're in the kitchen!"

After they had all had come out of the kitchen, the monks surrounded the small group and thanked them.

"Thank you Hop and Musubi. You saved us all," said one monk.

"Well, it wasn't just us," explained Hop. "We had help from some friends."

The monks turned to the others and gave them their thanks. Their master came out and the monks quieted down.

"Thank you for coming to save us," he said. "Please, stay for a while. We will have a big celebration for you tonight, in your honor."

Angel, Peanut, Mango, Dew, and Dust looked at each, then looked over to Hop and Musubi. "C'mon guys," said Musubi. "It'll be great."

Angel spoke up first. "Sure. Why not? It's been a while since I've been to a party."

"Let's party!!!" they all exclaimed.
