Dust hobbled into the village of Lai Chi, where the others had been waiting for him. "What took so long?" asked Blunt.

"I had a little run in with our...friends," replied Dust.

"You know we weren't supposed to fight them individually," exclaimed Creamy.

"Yeah, you idiot," said Toxic. Dust eyed him suspisciously. "C'mon. Tony's getting impatient."

Back at Shaolin Temple, Tony was informed of what happened to Dust. "Now they know we know where they are. One of two things will happen. Either they will wait for us to attack them, or they will come here and try to retake Shaolin."

"So what shall we do?" asked Extacy.

Tony thought for a few minutes. "Prepare the men. We're going to be receiving "guests"."

***

A group of six walked into the village of Lai Chi. They passed through without a word and without turning their heads. They walked on towards their goal. Once they reached the bottom of the steps of the Shaolin Temple, they stopped. There to greet them was none other than Toxic and Dust.

"Welcome to your execution," said Toxic. He had a huge smile on his face.

"YOU!!!" exclaimed Dew. "You'll pay for what you did to Mountain and Cloud!!"

Dust did a double take. To Cloud? he asked himself. Toxic laughed and long laugh. "I'll do the same to you as I did to them."

Dust was breathing hard now. Just as Toxic was about to strike, Dust grabbed him and hit three of his pressure points, paralyzing him.

"Dust!" yelled Toxic. "What the hell did you do?"

"I paralyzed you, you idiot," he replied. Dust turned to Hop. "Hop, I'm sorry about what happened earlier. I'd like to join you in your fight." He put out his paw.

Hop grasped his paw and shook it. "It would be an honor to fight alongside with you. Shall we?"

They ascended the stairs and were greeted by Creamy, Extacy, and Blunt.

"You won't be going much further than this," said Blunt. Then he saw Dust. "Dust, what the hell are you doing?"

"Taking you down!" he said right before he took Blunt out. Angel, Mango, and Dew went after Creamy while Dust and Peanut went up against Extacy. "Go after Tony!" Dust yelled to Hop and Musubi. They nodded and ran for the main hall.
