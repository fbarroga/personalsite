Chapter 6

Chicken Soup

 

Hop and Musubi made their way to the main courtyard of Shaolin Temple. There, waiting for them, was Tony the Tiger of the Danji Yi Clan.

"It's about time you got here," he said to them. "All this waiting's made me bored."

Hop and Musubi looked at each and nodded. They looked back at Tony and got into their ready stances.

***

Blunt sat up against the wall that he had been thrown towards by Dust. He was still feeling a bit woozy, and immediately fell back down as soon as he tried to stand. So instead, he sat and watched what was going on. Dust and Peanut had already taken care of Extacy. Peanut was tying him up while Angel, Mango, and Dew were fighting with Creamy. Creamy's long arms were giving them a difficult time getting in close to attack effectively.

Dust went over to help them. He sneaked up behind Creamy to try to take him by surprise. Unfortunately, Creamy was able to read the attack and block it. Fortunately for Dew, this gave him a chance to attack Creamy since he had left himself open. After that, taking him down was easy and he was soon tied up like Extacy.

Blunt started laughing maniacally. They all turned to look at him. "You may have turned on us Dust, but Tony will take care of the frog and the penguin. Then he'll take care of all of us! Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!!!!!"

Dew walked over to him and hit him in the head with his pole, knocking him out. "C'mon everyone," he said. "We have to go help Hop and Musubi." He ran towards the main courtyard with everyone following him.
