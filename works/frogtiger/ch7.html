Chapter 7

Fake Flowers

 

Dew arrived in the main courtyard first, but got thrown back when Musubi flew into him.

"Sorry Dew," said Musubi. "Are you okay?"

"Yeah, yeah, I'm fine," he replied. "How's Hop doing?"

"Well..." The others arrived in time to watch the rest of the fight between Hop and Tony.

The fight went back and forth until Hop got knocked down. "I have to go help Hop!" exclaimed Musubi.

Dew held him back. "No. We can't disturb their fight."

Hop rolled out of the way and Tony's attacked missed. Hop spotted an opening and hit Tony in a pressure point. Tony reeled back and growled in pain. Hop jumped up and started hitting Tony with everything he had until Tony had been knocked down. Hop picked him up and hit him in three of his pressure points, paralyzing him.

"Nooooo!" growled Tony. "I cannot be defeated!"

"Oh, but you can," said Hop. "And you have."

Everyone cheered and ran towards them. "So, what should we do with him now?" asked Dust.

They thought for a minute when finally Angel said, "Hey! Why don't we put him out front with Toxic?"

They all laughed. "That's a great idea," said Peanut. Dew and Dust picked up Tony and carried him down to the front gates and put him down next to Toxic.

"There we go," said Dew.

"Don't they look nice?" asked Dust. He and Dew went back up to the temple. Tony growled as they left.
