Prologue

 

It had been years, but the gates were finally beginning to weaken. The demons sensed this and had begun their assault hours ago.

They had just entered their third hour of their relentless attack on the gates of Avernus.

"Harder!" commanded a baatezu, a demon with a high status in the fiery realm.

The lesser demons were throwing their bodies against the gates until finally the hinges gave way and they fell to the ground. The demons all roared in glee as they poured through the gates.

The demons of Hell had been unleashed upon the Land of Mordor.
