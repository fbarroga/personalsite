Chapter 6

Second Wave

 

Lord Claune walked onto the battlefield that lay in before the castle gates and looked at the carnage that appeared before him. Many cloud giants had perished at the hands of the demons.

Ate, Cueball, Smirk, and Raziel stood a few meters from where Claune was standing, covered in black blood from the battle.

Ate looked over to him. "It looks like that's the last of them."

"Uhhhh...." said Raziel. Smirk ran over to Cueball and tugged on his robe. He looked at her and she pointed towards the edge of the clouds.

"I hope you have some reinforcements in the castle there, Lord Claune," said Cueball. "Because it looks like they just wanted to see how well we could hold up."

"Bring 'em on!!!" yelled Ate. The demon front line roared and began their charge.

"Raziel!" yelled Cueball. "Get into the castle onto one of the ramparts and sniper them from there." He nodded and ran with Lord Claune into the castle.

"Ready Smirk?" Ate asked. She nodded. A platoon of guards came out and joined them.

"We are ready to fight," said one of the guards. The others grunted in agreement.

Ate nodded to Cueball and Smirk. "Goooo!!!!!!" yelled Ate.

The group rushed forward to meet the new wave of attackers and began their stand there.

Hurry guys, thought Ate.
