Chapter 4

Reinforcements

 

Outside the temple, Ryoga set the groups. Cueball, Ate, Smirk, and Raziel were to go to the clouds and help the giants keep back the hellish hordes while the others were to accompany him in search of the dark goat, Zeth.

The group set to help the giants ran to the rainbow pool and made their way up to the top. Once there, they travelled along a very well hidden path to the guard's hut where the sounds of battle were taking place.

"Looks like you could use some help," said Ate, bashing three of the demons with his gigantic scimitar. The others armed themselves and began attacking the demonic warriors, trying to push them back and away from the floating island of Darbonne.

***

Meanwhile, the others searched the floating isle of Darbonne, looking for Zeth's dark temple. After searching for what seemed like hours, Charjer stumbled unto a hidden and booby-trapped path. They all entered the temple and found that they stood before the Hall of Insanity.

Charjer gasped as they walked through the hall. "What the hell?"

"It continues on with no end," explained Ryoga. "You'll go insane trying to find its end for it is said that that is where Zeth, the dark goat, lies."

"I don't think we'll find what we're looking for here," said Kechara. "Perhaps there is another temple?"

"I believe so," replied Ryoga. "Min?"

"I have heard rumors of another temple rising up somewhere in Lindon," he said. "But, like I said, that's just a rumor." Ryoga nodded.

"Only one way to find out," said Ryoga. In the blink of an eye, he vanished, with everyone following suit one by one.
