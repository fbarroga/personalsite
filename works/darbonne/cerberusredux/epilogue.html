Epilogue

 

The worn body of Kundur lay breathing in the beast's lair, all beaten and ravaged from the onslaught he had received from the three-headed dog Cerberus.

He was battered and bruised and wanted to leave, but the giant beast would not allow it. Not after what had been done to its children.

Though large in size, they were still pups. Now, revitalized and well nourished once again from their parent, the children surrounded the little orc bandit. Cerebrus lay patiently, watching as its children lay waste to the little Kundur.

His scream was heard throughout the Land of Mordor as the pups descended upon him.
