Chapter 4

On the Road Again

 

"I hate bears," said Kaerith as the group made their way through the Enchanted Forest. "Especially if they attack me."

"You've been attacked by a bear before?" asked Ate. Kaerith nodded in response.

"It was one of the most horrible experiences I've ever had." Raziel laughed. "Hey!" she said. "If you had a bear attack you, you wouldn't like it either."

"I hear that!" Ate and Cueball said at the same time.

A rumbling sound was heard behind the group after they said that, right behind of where Raziel was standing. A second later, a bear jumped out from the bushes roaring and attacked Raziel.

Sven watched for a few minutes before unsheathing his blade and killing the animal with one swift stroke.

"I bet you didn't enjoy that experience," said Kaerith before continuing to their destination.

Everyone else just giggled to themselves.
