Prologue

 

Heat washed over the room where the mighty dragon lord resides. He grows restless as he cranes his neck to look towards the entrance of his lair. His prisoner is trying to escape again. He lifts his four other heads and sends five balls of fire towards the helpless victim.

At the last second, he notices and dodges out of the way, barely escaping being burned to a crisp. He has no idea how long he's been down there. Escape looks impossible.
