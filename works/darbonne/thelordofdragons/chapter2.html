Chapter 2

Prestige

 

"Yes," answered Smirk.

"How many heads did you say it had?" asked Ryoga again.

"Five, I think," said Smirk. "I'm not sure, but I saw five fireballs coming straight at me."

Ryoga sat down.

"What is it?" asked Ate.

Sven answered first. "Only one dragon can shoot five fireballs at once."

"Tiamat," said Min.

"The five headed dragon?" asked Kaerith. "I thought that was just a legend?"

"You'd like to think that," said Ryoga.

"There are rumors of something in the forest west of Lindon," spoke Min. "Stories of deep roars and screeching have been born there as of late."

"So what do we do?" asked Cueball.

"Let's find Raziel," said Ryoga, standing up. "Then we'll see to those rumors."
