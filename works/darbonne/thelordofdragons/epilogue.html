Epilogue

 

The charred body of Raziel looked up in horror at the five headed fire breathing beast that loomed above him.

His scream was heard through the lands as the mighty lord of dragons devoured him with all five heads, limbs being torn apart from the body.

Then, just as if it never existed, the scream ceased and died away with the wind.
