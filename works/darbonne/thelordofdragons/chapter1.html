Chapter 1

Reverberating Sound

 

She saw a hall before her and she walked through it. Echoing all around her was a familiar voice.

"Help me!" it said. Then, all of a sudden, a hand appeared out of nowhere and grabbed her wrist.

"Smirk! Help me!" she heard while she was screaming. She turned and saw five fireballs coming straight at her.

She woke up screaming. Ryoga, Ate, Cueball, Kaerith, Sven, Min, Kechara, and Charjer were staring at her as well as everyone else in the first aid station. She kept screaming for another two minutes.

When she was finally out of breath, her screaming stopped and everyone went back to their business.

"You okay there Smirk?" asked Ate.

"Yeah," she replied. "Just had this weird dream of a five headed dragon. And somebody grabbed my arm. He sounded so familiar..."

"Did you say five headed dragon?" asked Ryoga.
