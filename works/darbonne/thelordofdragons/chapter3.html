Chapter 3

Check for Understanding

 

Everyone filed out of the first aid station in search of Raziel. All except Kechara and Charjer. They sat at the table, eating some food until Charjer spoke.

"So," he said, holding up a finger to quiet Kechara. "Ready to pay a visit to Tiamat?"

"No," replied a voice.

"This will be your greatest accomplishment. Just think: Raziel, slayer of the five headed dragon. Most powerful person in the Land of Mordor."

Raziel appeared from his hiding spot.

"Most powerful in the Land of Mordor, eh?" he asked. "I like the sound of that."

"Thought you might," said Charjer. Kechara chuckled.

Just then, everyone shuffled in.

"Hey!" boomed Sven.

"Where was he hiding?" Smirk questioned. Charjer pointed to the dark shadows next to the hearth.

"Well, then," said Ryoga. "Let's go."
