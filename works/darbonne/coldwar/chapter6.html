Chapter 6

Retaliation

 

Ryoga began his outline of their attack. "Gohan, you take Kechara and Charjer to the source of the cold in Puzzle Park. Cueball and Smirk will accompany Min back to the monastery. I will go with Ate and meet up with Sven. Make your way to Gowrie's chambers and we'll begin our attack."

Everyone nodded, got into their respective groups and ran off.

Upon arrival at Puzzle Park, Gohan, Kechara, and Charjer began their search for the source of the cold temperature. Finding it in a cave, they crawled through a small passageway and emerged through one of the walls into the icy cavern.

Min took Cueball and Smirk to the inner chambers of the monastery where it was the coldest. A wooden panel swung up, revealing the location of the eternal fires, which had been burned out. Going down, they made their way through a secret tunnel and scaled a wall down, which brought them high on the ceiling of the ice witch's chambers.

Ate and Ryoga met up with Sven at the Barbarian encampment. They made their way down the hall through the narrow crawl space and came up through the ground of the frozen chambers.

After regrouping, Ryoga made an ultimatum with the Ice Witch.

"Ice Witch," he said. She moved her eyes to meet his gaze. "Leave this place or prepare to be destroyed."

She cackled. "You, destroy me? How preposterous." Everyone equipped their weapons and Charjer moved up to the front, sensing an attack. "You will all be destroyed!" she yelled.

Shards of ice began forming in the air that grew to large icicles. They turned to point at the group and took flight towards them. Charjer deflected those icicles as a second wave approached them.

"There's too many!" he yelled. A smaller shard flew through the air and flew through his left arm, causing him to yell in pain.

"Attack!" yelled Ryoga.
