Epilogue

 

A huge beast moved towards a shadowy figure sitting on an ice cold throne. It kneeled.

"What is thy bidding, my queen?" it asked.

She looked at the huge beast. "Protect my sanctuary as I regain my strength, my balrog servant."

"As you wish, my queen."

The balrog walked out of her chambers and closed the door behind it.

"You have not seen the last of me Darbonne," she said quietly. "You shall see the return of Gowrie, and you shall all be destroyed," she yelled before she laughed insanely.
