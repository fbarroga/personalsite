Epilogue

 

High above the Land of Mordor where the gods dwell, Styx, Cirion, and Miphon looked down from the expansive library of Styx at the festivities.

"Interesting," observed Styx. "It seems like their thirst for exploration has sparked their interest in the great sea."

"That looks like a fine vessel," commented Miphon.

"I wonder if they'll make it to the island," wondered Cirion.

"We'll see," replied Styx.

With a wave of his hand, Styx created doves that flew down towards the port of Lindon, a sign of his blessing to the inhabitants of his world.
