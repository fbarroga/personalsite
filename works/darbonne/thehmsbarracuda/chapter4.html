Chapter 4

Why Are There So Many Choices?

 

The trio of Smirk, Kaerith, and Ryoga made their way through the food booths area.

"Oooohhhh!" whined Smirk. "There's so many good foods, I don't know what to get!"

"I know what you mean," said Kaerith. "Hey! Roc legs! Those are rare!" Smirk and Kaerith grabbed Ryoga and dragged him over to that booth.

"That leg looks like it'll feed all of us," observed Ryoga.

"Two legs!" yelled Smirk. Ryoga's jaw dropped. "Don't mess with a girl when she's hungry!" Kaerith giggled.
