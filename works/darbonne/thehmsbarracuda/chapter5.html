Chapter 5

Pretty Lights

 

"Man," said Gohan. "That's not fair." They were watching as the royal family were being served some food. Ate motioned one of the servers over and whispered something into his ear. The server nodded, straightened, and snapped his fingers.

Four servers, each with a try, approached Gohan, Min, Charjer, and Kechara and handed each a tray of food.

"Compliments of the royal family," one of the servers told them before returning to his duties.

"Right on!" Gohan said cheerfully before devouring the food on his tray.

As they ate, a fireworks display began, lighting up the darkening skies.

"I wonder where the others are," said Kechara. "They're going to miss the fireworks."
