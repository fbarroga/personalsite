Chapter 8

An Inside Look

 

"Wow," said Charjer emerging with Kechara from the lower decks. "This ship is huge." Kechara nodded in response.

Ryoga and Min emerged from the Captain's quarters. "That room is almost as large as the inside of the first aid station!" Ryoga exclaimed.

Smirk, Kaerith, and Gohan seemed to appear out of nowhere as Smirk yelled, "You should see how big the galley is!"

Ate and Cueball climbed down the main mast. "The view from up there is amazing," said Cueball.

"This is some ship," said Gohan.

"It's kind of exciting," added Kaerith. "I feel like we'll all be sailing on this ship to some sort of adventure." Everyone nodded.

"Hey!" exclaimed Ate. "Let's go check out the bridge." He lead the way with everyone trailing after.
