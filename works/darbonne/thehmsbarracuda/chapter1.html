Chapter 1

Of Course It'll Float

 

"Did you all hear the news?" Min asked to no one in particular as he walked into the first aid station. Ryoga, Smirk, Ate, Cueball, Kaerith, Gohan, Charjer, and Kechara were sitting at a table.

"What news?" asked Smirk.

"There's going to be a christening over at the port in Lindon. The Princes are supposed to be there!" Min said excitedly.

"Maybe you two could use your royal 'influence' to get us some good seats," Ryoga said to Ate and Cueball with a slight chuckle.

Ate and Cueball looked at each other. "Maybe," they replied together.

"Hey Min," called Charjer. "What time is this ceremony?"

"And will there be food?" asked Gohan. Everyone laughed.

"Yes, there will be food," replied Min. "And the ceremony is tonight."

"Tonight!" exclaimed Smirk. "Come on everyone! Let's get moving!"
