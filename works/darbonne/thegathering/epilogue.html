Epilogue

 

High above the floating island, Styx looked down from his grand library at his creation. A figure materialized on his left.

"Styx."

"Greetings Caledon," he replied.

"And greetings to you," said another person who appeared to the right of Styx.

Styx nodded to him. "Garth."

"You have been busy as of late," said Caledon.

"But, nevertheless, your creations are marvelous indeed," added Garth.

Styx nodded to both of them and they all looked down and smiled as their eyes washed over the floating island of Darbonne.
