Chapter 5

Forks

 

The group lead by Ryoga had finally arrived on the mystical isle. As they made their way along the rutted path, they arrived at a fork. A sign lay in front of the group that Ryoga couldn't read.

"Gohan," he called. "Can you read that sign?"

He looked at it and said, "No. I don't recognize the language."

"It loks dwarvish," Charjer spoke up. "The one pointing to the right looks like it says 'village' and the one pointing to the left says 'temple.'"

"Maybe we should split up," suggested Min. "The villagers may have some clues."

"Good point," replied Ryoga. "Min, Sven, and Charjer, come with me. We'll go talk to the villagers. Gohan, you take the others and see if you can pick up Kaerith's and Kechara's trail."
