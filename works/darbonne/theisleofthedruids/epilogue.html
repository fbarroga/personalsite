Epilogue

 

Everyone said farewell to Rhuddlan and made their way back to the village. On the way, they approached a large hole in the ground with giggling coming from inside of it.

The group looked down it to find Cueball, Ate, and Smirk giggling at Gohan trying to fend off Electra, who was on top of him, holding a skin of Uland rum.

"Don't just look!" complained Gohan. "Get her off!"

"It's not my fault I fell on you," said Electra.

"We'll be back in the morning," Ryoga said from above. Everyone following Ryoga chuckled as they heard Gohan's cries of help.
