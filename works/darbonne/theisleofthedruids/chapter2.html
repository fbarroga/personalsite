Chapter 2

Can You Add?

 

The group of Ryoga, Min, Sven, Ate, Smirk, Gohan, and Charjer made their way to the rainbow, which they would use to traverse from the floating island down to middle earth. As they made their approach, a cloaked figure appeared from behind a large boulder.

"What the?" Cueball exclaimed as he wielded his weapon in defense.

"Relax," said Ryoga. "I've asked Electra to accompany us on this journey."

Electra removed her hood and smiled at everyone. Gohan whispered silently to Charjer.

"She looks different when she's sober." Charjer nodded in agreement.

"Come on everyone," began Ryoga. "We must make haste."

The group continued on with Electra falling into step with Gohan and Charjer trailing after, chuckling all the way to middle earth.
