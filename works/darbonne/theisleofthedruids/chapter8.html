Chapter 8

Min, the Exorcist?

 

After receiving directions to the temple the sign pointed to, Ryoga and company made their way quickly to their destination. On the way, they came up to a large sign which Charjer deciphered that said 'Warning, Large Hole In The Ground.'

"Gee," said Min, "I hope the others didn't come this way."

As they continued on, they heard the growling and quickened their pace. They wielded their weapons as soon as they broke through the foliage. The mighty beast they had heard whirled around to face them.

As one, the four began their attack. Rhuddlan, Kiev, Kaerith, and Kechara emerged from the ruined temple.

"They're fighting the demon," observed Rhuddlan. "I can create a containment field around Fenrir."

Ryoga heard him. "Good! Prepare your spell!" The group fought on until a few moments later, Rhuddlan cast his spell.

"It is contained," said Rhuddlan. "This demon cannot harm anyone now."

"Demon?" said Min questioningly. "Maybe it can be put to rest." He went up to the demon beast and chanted. The demon howled as if in pain and its form started to dissipate until it was gone completely.

"Good," said Sven. "Let's go."
