Chapter 6

Hot on the Trail

 

Ryoga, Min, Sven, and Charjer arrived at the village and began inquiring about the recent rumors with Charjer acting as the interpreter. They eventually were lead to a seer. She told them the information they were seeking, then they proceeded to stock up on some supplies.

***

Gohan lead Ate, Cueball, Smirk, and Electra in the opposite direction into deep wilderness. As they journeyed, they came up to a large sign.

Oh no, thought Gohan. I can't read it!

"Hey Gohan!" called Cueball from the back. "What's that say?"

"Uh, I'm not sure," he responded. "Let's just keep going."

As they continued on their path, the ground suddenly gave way and they all fell into a large hole.
