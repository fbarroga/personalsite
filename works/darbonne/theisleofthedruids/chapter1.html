Chapter 1

A Scream in the Night

 

The sun was up and the first aid station was already buzzing with activity. Gohan, Charjer, Ate, Cueball, and Smirk were making small talk.

Ryoga rushed in suddenly with Sven and Min in tow. "We have to leave immediately!" he told everyone. "There's an emergency!"

"Where are we going?" asked Ate.

"To Angelesey, the druid's isle. There have been rumors of slayings there, the last one being last night."

"How do you know this?" asked Charjer.

"Kaerith and Kechara was told by some unknown source and left as soon as they heard," Ryoga explained. "They left a note at the monastery for Min and he told myself and Sven."

Gohan stood up. "Let's get going then!" The others who had been sitting rose up. Ryoga lead everyone out.

"And it was such a nice day today," complained Cueball before following everyone out.
