Chapter 7

The Horror... The Horror...

 

After they had all entered the protection of the temple, Rhuddlan offered Kiev, Kaerith, and Kechara a seat and some beverages. Once everyone was settled, he began his explanation.

"The recent attacks you've heard about," he started. "They are because of a demon wolf named Fenrir. It did all the destruction you see around you. It also killed the other druids here in this once sacred temple. I am all that is left of this order here on Angelesey. I was able to force it out of the temple and create a protective barrier against the demon."

"How did it appear?" asked Kechara.

"That, I don't know," he replied. "One day, there was a rumbling beneath the earth, then there were cries of pain."

Before he could continue, a loud growling was heard emanating from outside. Fenrir was staring at them all with deep crimson eyes.
