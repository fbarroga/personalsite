Prologue

 

Heavy rains were descending upon the land. The first aid station was not as busy on this day as it usually is any other day of the week.

Lounging about the first aid station were Cueball, Smirk, Kechara, and Charjer. It didn't seem as if they had any energy to even stand up to get a drink.

Lightning flashed and a low rumble of thunder could be heard as the small group lounged about in boredom. No one even seemed to remotely care when the sound of a cat getting runned over by something was heard from outside.

It just seemed like another rainy day in the Land of Mordor.
