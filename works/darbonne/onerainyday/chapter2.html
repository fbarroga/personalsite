Chapter 2

Soaked Like a Cat

 

No one could tell what time it was. The sun had not shone since the rain clouds had accumulated the night before. Time was temporarily lost to the inhabitants of the floating island.

Gohan walked in soaked through his armor. How long he had worn them was unknown, but guesses by the group were for a few hours because a horrid stench made its way quickly to the noses of everyone in the building.

"Ugh!" exclaimed Kaerith who had her back towards Gohan. "It smells like something died and started to rot."

Smirk and Kechara giggled at her observation. They were sitting opposite of Kaerith and could see the expression on Gohan's face at the last comment.

"Gee, thanks," he said, walking towards the table the three were sitting at. Kaerith's eyes went wide as she realized that the voice and smell were coming from the same person.

"Sorry, Gohan. I didn't realize it was you."

"It's okay. Where's Charjer?"

"He's over at the far end of the building helping with a leak," answered Kechara. "You know, you should really take a bath once in a while." Smirk giggled.

"Ha ha," Gohan said sarcastically. "For your information, I took one two weeks ago." The three made a face. Gohan moved towards the direction Kechara mentioned he could find Charjer at.

As he made his way to his destination, he heard someone remark, "Ugh, what is that stench?"

He turned into a doorway and found Charjer. "Found you."

"Oh," said Charjer, a bit stunned by the smell coming from Gohan. "Uh, what's up?"

"Come on," Gohan said excitingly. "I gotta show you something."

"Okay," said Charjer. Gohan quickly made his way to the exit with Charjer slowly following behind, holding his nose the whole way.
