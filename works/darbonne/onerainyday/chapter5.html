Chapter 5

Searching for the Cat Thrower

 

After Charjer had walked back into the first aid station, Gohan stood next to the fountain and thought for a while. A few minutes later, a group of cloaked figures walked past him into the first aid station. They made Gohan curious as to who they were, but he knew that he would most likely find out their identities later.

He searched all through the park area to the south of the fountain for suspects. After having questioned almost everyone and every animal he could find, he leaned against a wall that had a part that nudged him in his back. He turned and looked at the sign, which read 'Darbonne Park Zoo - Enter At Your Own Risk.'

Gohan shrugged and thought, Hmmm, I should try searching there. Couldn't hurt to try. And he entered the zoo.
