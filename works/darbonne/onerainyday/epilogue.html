Epilogue

 

Cueball and Ate took turns scrubbing Gohan's back as he bathed.

"That's the last time I peel a cat from your boot," complained Cueball.

"It wasn't my choice to throw it," retorted Ate.

"Yeah, well, you still stepped on it," Cueball argued back.

The others could hear their argument going back and forth and could do nothing but laugh.
