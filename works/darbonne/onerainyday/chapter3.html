Chapter 3

What the Hell was That?

 

Gohan lead Charjer outside of the first aid station into the pouring rain. As Charjer exited the building, he caught up with Gohan, who was waiting for him next to the large fountain.

"Well," began Charjer, "what was so important to show me out here that you couldn't back in there?"

Gohan reached down at something on the ground and picked it up. "This." He produced a smashed cat with blood and entrails still running out of it.

"What the hell is that?" asked Charjer.

"It's a dead cat."

"Well, I can see that."

"Someone threw this at me," complained Gohan.

"Why didn't you just throw it back at them?"

"Are you kidding? I can barely see you out here. How can I throw it at someone if I can't see out here?"

"What do you mean? I can see you perfectly," said Charjer.

Gohan sighed. "I guess I'm on my own in this one."

Charjer nodded. "And while you're looking for whoever threw that at you, take a bath. And don't carry the cat with you." That being said, Charjer walked back into the first aid station leaving Gohan staring back at him in the rain.
