Chapter 1

What Did He Just Say?

 

"Well, this is fun," Charjer said nonchalantly.

Cueball stood up in frustration. "I can't take this! It's too damn quiet." He grabbed his weapon which was leaning against the table he was sitting at and proclaimed, "I'm going to get some experience. Anyone want to come?"

At that moment Kaerith entered, leaving a trail of water as she made her way to an open seat next to Smirk. "You do not want to go out there," she said. "You can hardly see two meters ahead of yourself."

Cueball sighed. Charjer mentioned to him, "Hey, if the things you kill drop some gold, give me some."

"All right," he replied. Ate walked in as he answered.

"Where are you going, Cueball?" asked Ate.

"I'm going to get some experience. Wanna come?"

"Sure. Is anyone else going?"

"Not that I know of, unless they changed their minds," said Cueball. They both looked at everyone else that were sitting.

"Maybe later," said Charjer. The others agreed with him.

"All right," said Ate. "See you guys in a few hours." He and Cueball walked out into the rain.
