Chapter 7

Big Hole in the Wall

 

"Ow," said Charjer as Ate and Cueball helped him out of the wall he had been thrown into.

"Look!" exclaimed Smirk as she looked at the hole in the wall. "A hidden room!"

Min went in to have a look and came back out with the real Kaerith.

"Entiz," said Ryoga. "What's going on?"

"I'm sorry, my friend," apologized Entiz. "The witch came and threatened she would kill me if I told anyone."

"Don't worry, old friend. No one..." he paused as he looked at Charjer. "Okay, no one else was hurt."

"Thanks," grunted Charjer.

Ryoga nodded. "Entiz, if ever you need help, let me know."

"I will," he replied. "Thank you."

"Come on, everyone," said Ryoga. "Let's go."

Everyone walked out and started their way back to the floating island. They were passing through the Enchanted Forest when they heard a scream.

"What was that?" asked Ate.

"Maybe it was just the wind," guessed Min.

"Thousand gold pieces it's Tarnan," said Cueball to Ate.

"You're on!" agreed Ate and started running. Everyone trailed after him.

"He's fast for a guy with that much muscle," Kaerith said to Smirk. Smirk just grinned back.
