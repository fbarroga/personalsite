Chapter 5

Introductions

 

As the group walked through the city of Lindon, Smirk quickened her pace so that she was walking alongside of Kaerith.

"So, who are you really?" she asked her.

"I told you who I am already," she replied. She looked over at Smirk who was eyeing her suspiciously.

"Okay," said Smirk. She slowed down so Ate and Cueball could catch up to her pace. "I don't think she is who she says she is," she told them.

"I thought she was Kaerith?" said Ate with a rising intonation.

"She is, right Smirk?" asked Cueball.

"Only one way to find out," she replied with an evil grin on her face.

"We're here," said Ryoga as they approached an antique shop in the town of Lindon.
