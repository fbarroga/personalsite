Epilogue

 

Tarnan sat at his desk clutching the locket in his hand while brooding. She had been so close to him for two minutes before she began to lose her solidity and disappeared from his life again.

At the first aid station, the group had a feast prepared to help welcome Kaerith and to help her feel at home. Her voice caught the wind as she sang for everyone's entertainment.

The gods of Darbonne, though not visible to the eyes of mortals, were also in attendance as well as enjoying the festivities.
