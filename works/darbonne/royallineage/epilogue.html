Epilogue

 

Styx stood in his libary above Darbonne, overlooking the Land of Mordor, with Miphon standing to his left.

"Glad you could join us," said Miphon. A figure fizzled into appearance to the right of Styx.

"Greetings Cirion," said Styx.

Cirion smiled and replied, "Hello."

"The ranger sensed your presence," observed Styx. "He's more intuitive than the others I've seen."

Cirion nodded. "Perhaps I was moving too hastily."

"Perhaps," said Miphon. "But they all seemed to handle that situation very well." Styx and Cirion both agreed with a hearty grunt as they all stared down in the wondrous and vast Land of Mordor.
