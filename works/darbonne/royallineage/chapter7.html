Chapter 7

Land War Commence

 

Tioche stood atop the battlements in front of Prince Ragnar's castle. Prince Rutger along with Ate and the others walked up to the front line. Tioche met them as they approached.

"My liege. Come to watch the first attack?" The Prince nodded. The group behind him looked at each other.

"This can't be good," said Smirk.

"Begin the first wave," commanded Prince Rutger. Tioche nodded and yelled out the order.

"Stop!!!" yelled a voice from within the castle right before Tioche gave the command to begin firing arrows. Out rushed Ryoga, Cueball, Sven, Min, Raziel, Prince Ragnar, and Hodill and his platoon.

"I have something here that will change your mind," said Ryoga.
