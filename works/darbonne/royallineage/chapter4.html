Chapter 4

Ride to the Kingdom

 

The two units walked out of the first aid station eyeing each other. So much so that they walked through the doorway together and got stuck, causing the soldiers to draw their weapons and Cueball and Ate subduing them all so they could get outdoors peacefully.

Once outside, the two units stood opposing each other in their ranks.

"Summon the airship!" both Hodill and Tioche commanded at the same time. They stared each other down after they gave the command out.

Both airships arrived and landed on the opposite sides of the large fountain. "Charjer, Kechara, and Smirk, accompany Ate to the kingdom of Rutger," instructed Ryoga. "I will go with Sven, Min, and Raziel and join Cueball to the kingdom of Ragnar." Everyone nodded and went to their assignments.

Once everyone was on board, the airships lifted off and departed for their separate kingdoms. The trip was very quick and they both landed at approximately the same times.

Tioche escorted Ate and company to the throne room of Prince Rutger. As they walked, they were flanked on each side by rows of the Prince's soldiers in their finest uniforms.

"Wow," said Ate. "Fancy." Everyone chuckled.

When they had reached the throne room, Tioche announced them.

"May I present his royal highness, Ate...and company."
