Chapter 5

Dance with Your Shadow

 

As the group approached Sean Murdock's rum distribution building, Ryoga lead them to the back wall.

"I observed activity here during the last two nights. They have been sneaking in and out through a hole behind the middle bush."

"Sneaky little buggers," said Ate.

"So what now?" Sean asked impatiently.

"I think we should find out where they're taking the stuff," Charjer suggested.

"I agree," said Sven.

Charjer nodded. "Me and Gohan'll stay here. We'll try to stop these guys from stealing more rum."

"All right," replied Ryoga. "I'll take the others and travel to Uland Island. I've heard rumors of recent attacks there."

"Sounds good," said Gohan.
