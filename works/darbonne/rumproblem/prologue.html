Prologue

 

Sean Murdock, manager of The Spirited Spirits, supplier of rum to the floating island of Darbonne, was worried. Not because of the acid rains that was plaguing the Land of Mordor. Not because he keeps forgetting to lock his home when he leaves it for the day.

He was worried because he had not received any shipments of rum from Uland Island in two weeks to the day. Uland Island was where the rum distillery was located. It is at that location where the best rum in the Land of Mordor is brewed and distilled.

If he didn't receive a shipment soon, his customers would have his head hanging on a stake before the sun sets five days from now.
