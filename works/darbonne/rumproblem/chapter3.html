Chapter 3

Purveyor of Only the Best

 

"You've drunken away all my profits!" yelled the short stranger at the doorway. "You owe me money, woman!" he said loudly as he walked towards her.

"All right, all right," Electra replied, seemingly sobering up. "Let me stop by the bank to make a withdrawal."

"Oh no!" the stranger stopped her. "The last time you did that, you disappeared for three full moons! I'm not letting you go so easily this time!"

As they quarreled, Charjer spoke with the barkeep. "Who's the small guy?" he asked.

"That's Sean Murdock. He distributes the infamous Uland Rum in this location."

"The Sean Murdock?" The barkeep nodded. "I imagined he would be a bit...taller." Gohan chuckled.

"You owe me 1200 gold pieces for the two kegs of rum you took!" yelled Sean.

"I didn't take them, so I'm not paying!" Electra yelled back.

"Hey!" A new voice came from the doorway. "Bad time?"
