Chapter 4

The Plot Gets Frothy

 

Cueball, Ate, Sven, and Smirk were crowding around the outside of the doorway.

"How long have you all been out there?" asked Gohan.

"For a while now," replied Sven.

"We've been watching the quabble," added Ate.

"It was getting interesting until Sven spoke up," complained Smirk. Cueball laughed.

There was silence for a little while until Cueball spoke up. "So, what's going on?"

"She refuses to pay me for the rum she took!" Sean said, pointing at Electra.

"That's right," she agreed.

"Why not?" asked Gohan.

"Because I didn't take them."

"Then who did?" asked Ate.

"Orcs," Ryoga said, appearing suddenly from a darkened corner.

"Ryoga!" Ate, Cueball, Sven, and Smirk exclaimed together.

"Orcs?" Sean said, confused.

"Yes. I've been watching your store for the past two days. You have a bad case of orc infestation."
