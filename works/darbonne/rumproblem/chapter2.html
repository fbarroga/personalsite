Chapter 2

Who are You Calling Drunk Mr. Horse?

 

Gohan drew his sword in defense as a mug came flying at him. Apparently, Gohan and Charjer had walked into a tavern. The barkeep was yelling at the person who was throwing the mugs.

"You're going to have to pay for all of this!" yelled the barkeep at the mysteriously cloaked figure. The person giggled. Charjer and Gohan looked at each other in confusion.

"That sounded like a girl!" they said together.

"You're damn right!" acknowledged the barkeep. "And she's driven all the other customers away!" Charjer scanned the surroundings to find the claim to be true.

"So, who is that?" inquired Gohan.

"You don't know her?" the barkeep asked in response. Both rangers shook their heads. "This is the female ranger, Electra! She gets mean when she drinks."

"Electra?" Charjer thought out loud.

"The one and the same!" Electra said loudly.

At that moment, a shadowy figure appeared at the doorway. "You!" the stranger boomed. "It's your fault!"
