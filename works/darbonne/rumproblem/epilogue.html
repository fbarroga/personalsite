Epilogue

 

The pub was booming with customers. It was very crowded. Ryoga, Ate, Cueball, Sven, Smirk, Charjer, and Gohan were joined by Kaerith and Kechara.

"So," began Charjer to Kaerith and Kechara. "How was entertaining the orphans at the first aid station?"

"It was fun," replied Kaerith.

"I think they enjoyed the tricks I performed for them," Kechara added.

Electra plopped into a seat next to Ryoga with Sean Murdock following her.

"I told you I didn't take your rum," she said to him.

"All right, all right," Sean told her. "I believe you. You don't have to pay me anything."

"But you still owe me for all the mugs you destroyed and rum you drank," mentioned the barkeep.

Everyone laughed as they looked at the expression on Electra's face.
