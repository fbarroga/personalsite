Chapter 2

Things That Go Crunch in the Day

 

The others looked in the same direction when Charjer said, "I don't believe it..."

Smirk screamed and yelled, "ZOMBIE!!!" That caused Ate and Sven to act on impulse and they both delivered a shoulder block to Raziel together, causing him to fly out of the building.

As Charjer was trying to calm Smirk down, everyone heard a very audible crunch sound as a carriage came to a halt. Out stepped Cueball, returning from a trip to his father's castle. Gohan followed in his wake.

"Do you smell that Gohan?" asked Cueball as he sniffed the air on their way into the first aid station.

"Yeah, smells like burnt lizard," said Gohan, trailing after Cueball. They greeted everyone and sat down and wondered what the big commotion was. Sven answered.

"I think you ran over Raziel's crispy body." They all looked out at Raziel's crushed and burnt remains under the wheels of the carriage.

"Huh," breathed out Cueball. "Who would've thought?" he asked, looking at Gohan with a raised eyebrow. Gohan harumphed and placed a medium-sized pouch of gold coins into Cueball's waiting hand. Everyone snickered.

"Anywho," Sven said all of a sudden. "Tell us about your nightmare Charjer."

As Charjer was about to begin, Ryoga entered the first aid station. "Nightmare?" he inquired.

"Yes," responded Ate. "Charjer was about to engage us in his tale."
