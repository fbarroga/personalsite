Chapter 9

Scrambled Eggs

 

"Now how are we supposed to beat them?" asked Zit. Everyone hit him.

Angel reached into his back pocket and pulled out six blunts. He handed one to each of them. "Take a puff," he told them.

They all took a long hit, especially Mari. There was a loud rumble from outside. They all ran out of the bathroom and looked. A huge robot that looked like it was made from bongs was being formed. Mari took another hit and disappeared.

"Where did Mari go?" asked Block.

"She got transported into the mighty 'Extendo Zord'. Take another puff and you'll teleport inside it, too," Angel told them. So they all took a puff and disappeared.

They reappeared inside the Extendo Zord and found Mari looking around.

"This is so cool," she said.

"She just had two hits and she's already stoned," said Dam.

Zit looked out the monitor. "Uh, guys, I think we might be in trouble."

They all went to look and saw the Energy Save Plus slowly advancing toward them. "Everybody, get to a station," Block shouted.

Block looked at all the buttons and blinking lights at his station. "Do something Block!" Kiss yelled.

"What the fuck do I look like? A power ranger? I don't know how to use this thing," Block yelled back.

Mari pushed a button and smoke began filling the cockpit. It turned out to be weed smoke. "Whoa, this is some good shit," said Mari.

The Energy Save Plus were inching they way along, getting closer each second. Dam saw them approaching and laughed. He leaned on the panel in front of him. While he was laughing, he slipped and his head hit the self-destruct button. "Whoops," he said when the alarm started blazing.

Zenith and Sony saw the smoke coming out of the top of the Extendo Zord and looked at each other before continuing on.

"Oh shit," said Block. "I think we should get out."

"Okay," said Zit. He leaned on the window next to him and fell out.

"Hey, that's a good idea," said Kiss and followed Zit out the window.

One by one they fell out. Zenith and Sony finally made it to the Extendo Zord and finally heard the alarms. They looked at each other and said, "Oh shit." The Extendo Zord blew up, taking the Energy Save Plus with it.
