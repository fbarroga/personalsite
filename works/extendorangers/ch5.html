Chapter 5

Please Stop Hitting On Me

 

Angel started to apologize then gasped. His search for the six had finally ended.

"Hello," said Angel. They all just stared at him. For a few minutes, all they did was just look at each other.

"Okay, this is just a little awkward," thought Angel. So he tried again. "Hi. My name is Angel. I have been searching for you all."

Mari, Azure, Kiss, Dam, Zit, and Block looked at each other. They all looked confused.

Angel took a puff out of his pipe. "Please, we have very little time. Follow me," he said.

He walked up the street. Reluctantly, they all followed Angel.

He took them to an abandoned warehouse. "Spiffy," he thought. Then Angel turned around and addressed the group.

"Everyone, my name is Angel. I have been searching for you for," he looked at his watch, "about a half hour now."

"Why were you looking for us?" asked Zit.

Block slapped him in the back of his head. "You idiot!" he said. "It's obvious this guy is gonna hook us up."

"Cool," said everyone all at once.

"Yes, that is exactly what I am going to do," replied Angel. He reached into his back pocket and pulled out six syringes.

"All right!" exclaimed Block.

"With these syringes, you will turn into the Mighty Morphine Extendo Rangers. The protectors of free speech and self expression..."

"And free weed," said Mari.

"Go on," said Angel. "Give it a try. Just stick yourself as if you were shooting up."

They all looked at each other, then stuck the needles into their arms and injected themselves with the morphine.

The transformation took place. They could all feel the power surging through their bodies.

Block became the White Extendo Ranger, wielder of the baton.

Azure transformed into the Spiral Extendo Ranger who used an umbrella.

Zit turned into the Plaid Extendo Ranger and carried a backpack.

Dam became the Pastel Extendo Ranger and wielded a purse.

Kiss morphed into the Leather Extendo Ranger and carried a whip.

Mari turned into the Black Extendo Ranger and packed a 12-gauge.

A new team of Mighty Morphine Extendo Rangers was born.
