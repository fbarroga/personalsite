Prologue

 

Angel was on the run again. Unfortunately, those whom he had chosen to wield his incredible powers had been killed by the seemingly invincible dastardly duo Zenith and Sony, the "Energy Save Plus".

After failing to find the right people, he ran before the battle was even finished.

So here he was again, on the run. Hopefully, he would find the ones he was searching for, before they got to him.
