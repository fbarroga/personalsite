Chapter 2

Oregano is Not a Good Substitute for Marijuana

 

Angel made his way to the city limits. He looked at the sign: Honeydew.

As he approached the toilet inspector building, he stopped to ask for directions. He got odd looks from them as he asked where the sperm bank was. Then he thought, "Why am I looking for the sperm bank? I should be searching for the six." So he started to walk towards the town.

He looked down and saw his pipe start to glow. It seemed to glow brighter as he faced to the northeast. So he walked in that direction.

After a few steps, Angel started to feel a warm sensation. He looked down to see that his shirt was on fire. Apparently, he had lit the pipe that he hung around his neck and forgot all about it when he was asking for directions.

After much fanning, which only made the fire bigger, he managed to put the fire out by rolling back and forth on dried grass.

As the fire spread behind him, Angel made his way further into town.
