Chapter 6

Moose Jaw is a Delicacy

 

Zenith and Sony were in the town of Honeydew looking for Angel. He had somehow managed to lose them. How he did it they didn't know since the town itself wasn't very large.

They had been searching for an hour before Sony started picking up high levels of morphine. The traces were coming from the East, so they quickly set off in that direction.

As they were walking, people were pointing and laughing at them. Sony looked towards Zenith and saw a pigeon on his head taking a shit. He tried shooing it away only to have it land on his head and take a shit on him.

Zenith got fed up and fired his finger lasers, vaporizing the pigeon. Everyone ran for their lives. The Energy Save Plus continued towards their destination.
