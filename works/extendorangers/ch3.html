Chapter 3

The Chocolate Covered Easter Bunnies

 

In the shaded alley on the side of the toilet inspector building, the Energy Save Plus watched as Angel made a fool out of himself.

They had been searching for him since he had departed from Toasted Rice Tea Island before their last battle had even finished.

When Angel had lit his pipe, Zenith and Sony had picked up the residual traces that it left behind. They arrived in mere seconds using their portable teleportation devices.

Now they were waiting for the right moment to attack. "Soon, we will be victorious," they thought.
