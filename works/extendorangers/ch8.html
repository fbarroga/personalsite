Chapter 8

Shooting Blanks

 

The Extendo Rangers made their way to the park, which is the last place you would want to go to if you were trying to hide from people who were trying to kill you.

After slapping Angel repeatedly, they were finally able to wake him up. "Who were those guys that attacked us?" asked Kiss.

Angel told them everything. He explained who the Energy Save Plus were, his escape from Toasted Rice Tea Island, the deaths of the previous teams of Extendo Rangers, and his unending search for the right team to help him destroy the Energy Save Plus.

"So how the fuck are we supposed to beat those guys if they're cyborgs?" asked Mari.

"I know you can. I have faith in you," replied Angel.

"Extendo Rangers!" They all turned around. Across the park were Zenith and Sony. They both were very mad.

"You'll pay for blowing our balls off Black Extendo Ranger!" yelled Zenith. Both he and Sony pressed a button on their right temples and grew ten times their size. They both opened fire on them.

The Extendo Rangers dodged the shots and made their way to the bathrooms, which would provide them shelter for a few minutes.
