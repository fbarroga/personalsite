Chapter 9

Donde Esta El Cuarto De Bano?

 

A half hour later, the car that carried Roberto Stalin, Ben Dover, and Princess Xiaoyu arrived at President Manuel Labor's second private landing strip.

"Come on Mr. Dover," said Stalin.

"Sir, where are we going?" asked Ben.

"North, my friend. We are going north," he replied. Stalin boarded the plane after Ben and the princess got on and prepared for take-off.

***

It took the better part of an hour for Kusomaki, Tiffany, and Bella to arrive at the Presidential Mansion. When they finally did get to their destination, many of the security guards were standing at the gate. Stalin had called President Labor to let him know that Kusomaki, Tiffany, and Bella were going to be "stopping by".

It doesn't look like they'll let us in without a fight, thought Kusomaki. The car came to a stop at the gate and they got out. Kusomaki looked over at Tiffany and Bella and nodded. They all looked toward the guards and in one swift motion, Tiffany and Bella pulled out their weapons and started firing on them. Kusomaki came up between them with his light saber and moved ahead a little to help deflect the shots being fired at them.

After dispatching all the guards at the gate, they entered the mansion. They quickly made their way up to President Labor's chambers, where he was found cowering under his desk. Kusomaki finally got him to come out after threatening to destroy his mansion.

The interrogation began.

"Where did Stalin take the Princess?" asked Kusomaki. Manuel just looked at him. Tiffany came up from behind and put one of her gun to his head and cocked it. He broke down.

"He took her to Toronto. In Canada," he replied.

"Where in Toronto?" pressed Kusomaki.

"I don't know!! I swear!"

Kusomaki looked toward Bella. "Make sure he won't be able to contact anyone or leave." Bella nodded. She secured him to the bars on the window, then pushed him out.

"Ready?" asked Tiffany. Kusomaki and Bella nodded and walked out to the car with her. It was time to stop Stalin.
