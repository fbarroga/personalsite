Chapter 12

Dead End: Game Over

 

Kusomaki had it well planned. Rose Bacon would wait outside to greet him and lead him into the building. Waiting just inside would be Pina Sausage. Backing her up would be Bella Donna and Tiffany Svendish. Kusomaki himself would be waiting underground in the facility if he somehow managed to get past them. It was time to put that plan into motion. It was nearing the meeting time. And hopefully, this is where it would all end.

At one p.m. exactly, Roberto Stalin, Ben Dover, and Princess Xiaoyu arrived at the stadium. Rose was outside waiting for them to arrive. After a brief greeting, she lead them to the door. They entered and were greeted by Pina.

"Miss Sausage!" exclaimed Stalin. "What are you doing here?"

"Stopping you." She pulled out her guns and started firing at them. Stalin dived to the left while Ben with the Princess dived to the right. Stalin pulled out his laser, got up and started firing back. Pina stopped firing and dived to her right. Stalin kept on firing and started moving forward. As he shortened the distance between himself and Pina, he was greeted by a hail of gun fire to either side of him courtesy of Tiffany and Bella.

Stalin dived down for cover again. He fired in Tiffany's direction first. After Stalin fired a few shots towards her, gunfire stopped coming from that direction. Firing in the other direction, Stalin managed to stop the barrage of bullets coming from that direction as well.

Stalin looked back at Rose and smiled. "Come on Mr. Dover. Let's go." Ben got up with the Princess and followed Stalin to the back. After making their way to the backstage area, Stalin proceeded to the switch and lifted it, opening the door. They made their descent.

At the bottom, Stalin was shocked. "Kuso-sama," he said. Kusomaki turned to face him.

"This is where I stop you Mr. Stalin," he replied. Kusomaki pulled out his light saber and turned it on.

"Very well. It has been a while since I've been in a good fight. Give me all that you have." With that said, Stalin pulled out his own light saber and also turned it on. His blade was crimson red. They both got in their ready stances. Ben moved back slowly, then ran up the stairs. The Princess watched him run away, then heard gunfire.

Good riddance, thought Stalin. He made the first move. Kusomaki blocked it and hopped back. They started one of the greatest sword fights in the world. Stalin and Kusomaki fought across a catwalk that stretched the entire way of the underground warehouse. As they made their way to the end of the catwalk, Kusomaki swung at Stalin. While he was still turned around, Stalin stabbed him in the lower abdomen.

"KUSOMAKI!!!!!!" yelled Princess Xiaoyu. Stalin turned off his light saber, causing it to withdraw from Kusomaki's body. Kusomaki dropped to his knees, then fell to the ground. Stalin looked down at him, then the top half of him, cut diagonally from his left shoulder to the right side of his waist, slid off his body. The rest of it fell backwards.

Tiffany, Bella, Pina, and Rose came down the stairs. Tiffany, Bella, and Pina were all injured. They all saw the aftermath of the battle. Rose went to the end of the catwalk to check on Kusomaki while the others escorted the Princess out of the building and back to China.
