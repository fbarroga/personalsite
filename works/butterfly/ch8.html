Chapter 8

Konnichi Waaaaaaa!!!!!!

 

Roberto Stalin, accompanied by Ben Dover, his two guards, and Princess Xiaoyu left the Presidential Mansion quite early the next day. They left around the same time that Tiffany and Kusomaki took off from his landing strip back in Russia. They were going to a small airport in Acapulco and left the mansion a little early.

Before going to the airport, they stopped by a little shopping square to pick up a few things before departing. What they didn't know was that Kusomaki, Tiffany, and Bella were also there looking around. They slowly got closer to each other.

Finally, without knowing it, they met at opposite sides of the fajita stand. Bella and Ben saw each other and each pulled out their guns and started firing. Tiffany and Kusomaki ducked down as did Stalin with the princess. Bella took out the two guards while Ben just hit two bystanders.

"Do you know her?" Stalin asked Ben.

"Yeah. I met her in Interpol. She seems to be looking for you."

"I see," said Stalin.

"Where do you know Ben Dover from Bella?" asked Kusomaki.

"He used to be my partner in Interpol. I turned my back on him and he hit me while I was turned around. I'm going to kill him until he is dead," Bella replied.

"Allow us to help," said Tiffany. She pulled out her guns and Kusomaki pulled out his katana. "Ready?" she asked Kusomaki and Bella. They both nodded.

Bella and Tiffany jumped up and started firing while backing up. Kusomaki moved behind them, his katana in front of him to deflect shots. Ben also got up firing as did Stalin, except for the fact that he was using a laser instead of a gun. Kusomaki tried deflecting the beam with his katana. It was broken in two from the blast.

"Damn," said Kusomaki. He threw what was left of his katana to the side and reached to his side. "It's a good thing I picked this up back in Russia." He took out a light saber. He turned it on, which produced a blue photon blade. They all looked at it. Kusomaki then said, "Are you two ready?" They nodded in response.

Kusomaki stood up, light saber in front of him, and found himself facing Stalin. Ben, holding the princess, stood up slowly to his right. Bella and Tiffany also got up slowly, slightly behind and to either side of Kusomaki. Stalin moved to his right and Ben and the princess moved with him. Kusomaki, Bella, and Tiffany mirrored him.

Kusomaki looked further on in the direction they were travelling. He's moving to his car, he thought. He jumped onto the stand and tried to make it across.

"Go!" Stalin yelled to Ben. Ben ran to the car, shoved Princess Xiaoyu in, then got in himself. Stalin fired a shot at Kusomaki, who deflected it. He ran to the car. Bella and Tiffany started firing at him. Unfortunately, all their attempts at trying to hit him failed as he made it to the car.

Kusomaki, Bella, and Tiffany made it to the dust cloud the car left behind in time to see the car disappear around the corner of a building. Bella swore. "We were so close," she said. Kusomaki and Tiffany looked at her. "C'mon, back to the car."

"Why? Where are we going?" asked Tiffany.

"I think I may know who he came to see here in Mexico," replied Bella and led them back to the car.
