Chapter 4

Lunch Encounter

 

The day was not yet over and it had been very eventful so far.

Not only was she stinking rich and had met Jeebus, but she also got her fill of animal loving. Well, at least for the day. Now, it was time for lunch. Hugh couldn't decide what to eat, so she went outside and fucked her pig again.

After getting dressed, she noticed someone watching from the bushes, so she flashed the person. Then, she heard a loud clunk. She ran over to the bushes and found that the Burger King had been spying on her. Apparently, he had fainted when she flashed him.

She picked him up and carried him into her house.

The king woke up finding himself tied to a chair. "Hmmm, kinky," he thought to himself. That was when he saw Hugh.

"So, you were the one who told everyone I liked fucking my pig. Why did you do that?"

"I will never tell you." He glanced over at Crispy very quickly. That was when Hugh knew what the king wanted.

"You came here for Crispy! Am I right?"

The Burger King broke down in tears. "Yes, yes I did."

"Is that why you were spying on me? Because you wanted Crispy?"

"Yes. But it's not what you think. When I first saw you fucking your pig, I fell in love."

Hugh lit up. "You fell in love with me?"

"No, with your pig."

Hugh looked down. "Oh."

"I want your pig. I must have him. Please, whatever the cost, I will pay it."

"Sorry, but Crispy is not for sale."

"How does ten million gold pieces sound?"

Cha-ching!! "Sold!"

"Good. Now, would you mind untying me?"

"Oh. Sorry." Hugh untied the Burger King, then said her goodbyes to Crispy.

The king left with the pig and Hugh thought about their new life together. "Hmmm, kinky," she thought.
