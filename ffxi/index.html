Here I am on the ship from Mhaura heading back to Selbina. I didn't get a picture on the way to Mhaura, but I figured this'd be nice anyway with the main mast in the background.

This is on the same ship and I decided to take a touristy picture with the helmsman. Fortunately, he didn't seem to care.

I thought I'd just add this one in for fun. I was fighting a land crab in The Fumaroles and it started backing up into a rock until it went up to make it look like it was floating. For some reason, it reminds me of Metroid.

This is a waterfall that can be found in North Gustaberg. If you do the quest called "The Gustaberg Tour" in Bastok with your very first job, this is the first time you'll see this waterfall. It's really nice, especially during the daylight hours.

This is my home town, Bastok. The fountain in the background is in the markets district and on the opposite side of the photo is the auction house. This district is always full and totally lags during any event, like the one we had this past easter.

Not many people know that there's another beach in Valkurm Dunes. I won't give up the location out here. In the game, I might show someone where it is, but a secret's always a secret. Anyway, this is a shot of the nice foliage that's there.

Here's another shot of the secret beach, but this time looking out at the ocean. I got a really good picture of a CG wave rolling in.

I finally made it to Jeuno. For those of you who don't know, Jeuno is a neutral country in the land of Vana'diel. You know, kind of like Switzerland. This is a picture of me in front of the Palace of the Grand Duchy. Pretty spiffy.

I bet a lot of people don't take the time to look around a town very much. This is a monument in Jeuno dedicated to the memory of the Crystal Wars. It's supposed to represent the unity of the three nations in Vana'diel: Bastok, San d'Oria, and Windurst. Of course, Jeuno is part of it, but, like I said above, they're neutral.

I finally got to take my picture next to a bogy. Luckily, a high level WHM took it on, so I was able to move in and take the screenshot. Right on.
